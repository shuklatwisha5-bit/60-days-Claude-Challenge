Key Learnings
Role-Based Prompting significantly changes AI responses.
Different personas provide unique perspectives.
Founder personas focus on outcomes and business value.
Developer personas focus on implementation and technical excellence.
Using a specific role makes AI outputs more relevant and actionable.
