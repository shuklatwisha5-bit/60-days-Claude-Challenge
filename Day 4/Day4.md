## Objective

---

## What is Chain-of-Thought Prompting?

Chain-of-Thought (CoT) Prompting is a technique where an AI model is encouraged to reason through a problem step-by-step before producing a final answer. This helps generate more accurate, structured, and logical outputs.

### Benefits
- Improves reasoning ability
- Produces detailed responses
- Breaks down complex problems
- Helps with planning and decision-making
