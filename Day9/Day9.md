The goal of Day 9 was to understand how experienced AI builders create applications using an iterative development approach. Instead of asking AI to generate a large and complex application in one prompt, we first build a working Minimum Viable Product (MVP) and then improve it through additional focused prompts.

📚 What I Learned
MVP First: Build a simple, functional version before adding advanced features.
Iterative Development: Enhance the application step by step using multiple prompts.
Claude Artifacts: Generate and download real interactive HTML applications.
AI Product Building: Follow the same workflow used by professional developers—build, test, refine, and improve.

💡 Key Takeaways
AI-generated applications become more reliable when built incrementally.
Breaking a large task into smaller prompts improves output quality and consistency.
Iterative prompting closely resembles real-world software development practices.
Testing and refining after each stage leads to a better final product.
🚀 Conclusion

Day 9 demonstrated the importance of iterative AI development. By first creating a working MVP and then progressively enhancing it, I was able to build a more polished and feature-rich application. This approach not only improves the quality of AI-generated outputs but also reflects the workflow followed by experienced AI product builders.
